# CareerTrellis product roadmap

Updated October 24, 2026. Owner: Hunter / CareerTrellis product and engineering.

Continue as a browser-based job-search product while testing whether its ease of
use, search results and application materials justify ongoing development. The
[comparison with ai-job-search](docs/AI_JOB_SEARCH_ASSESSMENT_20261019.md) supports
selective adoption rather than replacing the hosted platform. It does not yet
establish comparative performance or commercial demand.

The [October 24 backlog checkpoint](PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md#october-24--evidence-checkpoint-before-the-next-application-study)
records merged foundations and remaining acceptance. The
[October 23 pilot](docs/value-evaluation/PILOT_20261023.md) provides partial
source-level application evidence; it leaves the comparative decision open.
Next, deliver real cover-letter PDFs, review and freeze the next candidate and
native workflow, then obtain the complete-batch funding decision before paid
execution. Score completed native output before human corrections; report
quality, native-policy compliance, cost, time and effort separately. Discovery
and consenting-user studies remain later, separately scoped work.

## Delivery sequence

| Stage | Work | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Establish the comparison | VALUE-001 protocol; reuse SEARCH-002 and AI-004 controls; DOC-001 documentation reconciliation | Fixed inputs, scoring rubric, thresholds and exact revisions; known gaps explicit before execution |
| 2. Address core quality | Bounded SEARCH-003/005 corrections and SEARCH-004 explanations; QUALITY-001 critique and QUALITY-002 final-document verification | Synthetic checks first; then authorized comparable output and discovery measurements, including editing effort and total cost |
| 3. Decide continuation | Complete VALUE-001 target-user observations and owner review | Continue, narrow/revise, or stop decision based on useful results and unaided workflow completion |
| 4. Expand only where justified | QUALITY-003 continuity, QUALITY-004 writing preferences; revisit managed AI, billing and additional providers | Evidence of unmet user needs and a sustainable cost/support model; normal separate rollout approvals |

Take bounded slices; stage 2 can address a diagnosed issue while stage 1 prepares
the comparison. Do not use new features to postpone the decision indefinitely.
Define the comparison scope and review point before participant or paid runs.

## Decision rules

- Continue when target users can complete the browser workflow with materially
  less assistance and useful results, while factual fidelity and document quality
  meet the predeclared floor at acceptable operating cost.
- Narrow or revise when one core gap has a plausible bounded remedy; specify the
  remedy and next evaluation before expanding again.
- Prefer upstream for personal use, or stop broad product expansion, if the
  browser experience offers insufficient benefit to justify development/support.
  This decision does not authorize service retirement or user-data migration.
