## Budget and evidence boundaries

The initial authority was $25 total and $2 per complete packet attempt. Reservations
use free token counting, a conservative input allowance, maximum output tokens and
a margin. No automatic retries occur. Unknown outcomes retain full reservation and
pause new spending until reviewed operational reconciliation. These estimates do
not mathematically guarantee provider billing bounds.

At the three-case checkpoint, known usage-based list-price cost is $3.828030.
Two original CareerTrellis charges remain unknown with $0.647290 fully reserved;
combined exposure is $4.475320 and aggregate actual cash cost remains unknown.
Upstream packet costs are V01 $1.248904, V02 $1.238148 and V03 $1.301656.
V01/V02 stopped before next paid dispatch at projected exposure $2.114114 / $2.015073.
V03's last charge settled before a tool-policy refusal. Its cost is known; its
workflow is incomplete. A generic loopback 403 message is not provider-auth failure.

The owner subsequently approved $4 for future packet attempts while keeping the
combined $25 total. Earlier failures and unknowns remain retained. Any new accounting
phase must retire the old spending route, bind its unchanged configuration and
remaining exposure, share one new allocation across both products and deny old
packet replays. A higher limit is not permission to reset the total or rerun a
stopped packet. This document does not claim that transition has been activated.

The source-level pilot does not measure unaided setup, authenticated download,
active human editing, end-to-end participant latency, live discovery quality or
ongoing support cost. All-case completion, median editing and p90 latency remain
unavailable. Missing evidence fails the corresponding floor; it is not a pass.
